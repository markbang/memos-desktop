# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and precise. Contributions should focus on the problem and its evidence, not on people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and disruptive conduct in issues, reviews, or community channels.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Serious or repeated incidents may be reported to GitHub.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's security contact or GitHub profile.
